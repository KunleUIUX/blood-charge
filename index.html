Blood Charge

Give blood near you.

Find a centre in Lagos, book a time and see which patient your blood helped.

Read the case study

A concept app for iPhone and Android.

Most people have never been asked to give blood. In my survey, every person who hadn't given said they never had the chance.

100%of non-donors never had the chance
80%would give to anyone who needs it
51%had no centre nearby, or weren't sure

What it does.

Know if you can give today.

Home tells you when your 12-week wait is over.

Requests matched to your blood type.

You only see requests you can help with, sorted by distance.

A nurse can come to you.

Free home visits in Lagos, tracked on a map.

Your ticket in Apple Wallet.

Show the QR code at the front desk when you arrive.

Points for every donation.

Turn them into free blood counts and pharmacy vouchers.

Know what happens first.

Short videos and a checklist for your first donation.

Safe and private.

Matched by blood type

You only see requests your blood can help. An O+ donor never sees an O− request.

Checked by hospitals

A request goes live only after the hospital confirms it.

Your number stays private

Hospitals see your phone number only if you pledge to their request.
